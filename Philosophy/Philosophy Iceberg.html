The Philosophy Iceberg

From the sunlit surface of introductory concepts to the crushing abyss of CCRU hyperstition, this lesson descends through 7 levels and 113 concepts. Each idea gets full treatment: core explanation, three examples, analogy, misconception, and why it matters.

What You'll Learn

1. The Surface — Foundations of Western Philosophy

Rationalism vs. empiricism, solipsism, the JTB theory of knowledge, nihilism, realism, stoicism, theism/atheism/agnosticism, and Berkeley's idealism.

2. The Shallows — Ethics, Mind, and the Classic Debates

Hume's fork, cogito, property dualism, anti-natalism, free will vs. determinism, absurdism, deontology, utilitarianism, virtue ethics, Marxism, and Plato's Forms.

3. The Descent — Existentialism, Psychoanalysis, and Critical Theory

Sartre's radical freedom, Hegel's dialectic, Schopenhauer's Will, Heidegger's ready-at-hand, Freud's Oedipus complex, structuralism, and the Frankfurt School.

4. The Deep — Post-Structuralism and the Birth of Theory

Accelerationism, Derrida's deconstruction, Foucault's disciplinary society, Bataille's accursed share, Baudrillard's hyper-reality, and Deleuze & Guattari's rhizomes.

5. The Abyss — Bataille, Land, and the Weird

Solar economics, blockchain time, the political compass as hyperstition, base materialism, gender accelerationism, and god-as-lobster.

6. The Void — CCRU, Hyperstition, and Capital as AI

Thanatos accelerationism, libidinal materialism, the Lemurian time war, hypervirus, and Land's claim that capitalism and AI are identical.

7. The Unfathomable — Esoterica at the Bottom of the Iceberg

Neo-Leviathans, the pneumogram, Z/Acc, the CCRU's non-existence, Hasan-i Sabbah as a line of flight, and the Hegel greentext.

Next Steps

Where to go after the iceberg.

Read the Primary Texts

Start with Descartes' Meditations, Hume's Enquiry, and Plato's Republic. Then move to Kant's Critique of Pure Reason.

Follow the CCRU Thread

Nick Land's Fanged Noumena, the CCRU's Writings 1997–2003, and Sadie Plant's work on cybernetics.

Deleuze & Guattari

Anti-Oedipus and A Thousand Plateaus — difficult but foundational for everything in Levels 4–5.

Bataille's Trilogy

The Accursed Share, Story of the Eye, and Inner Experience — transgressive philosophy at its limit.

Baudrillard

Simulacra and Simulation and Fatal Strategies — for hyper-reality and the object's revenge.

Secondary Sources

Gregory B. Sadler (YouTube) on Hegel, CCK Philosophy on post-structuralism, and Matt McManus on the Hicks/Peterson debate.
