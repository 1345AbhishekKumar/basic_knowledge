Quadratic Attention vs. Kimi Delta Attention

Why standard Transformers scale quadratically as $O(N^2)$ and how Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) replaces all-to-all pairwise calculation with an $O(N)$ linear running mean.

Detailed Architectural Explanations

Mathematical Mechanism

The Running Mean Analogy

Imagine calculating the average height of 10,000 people. You do not need to re-sum all 10,000 numbers every time a new person walks in. You simply maintain a running sum and count, updating it with the delta in $O(1)$ time. Kimi Delta Attention applies this exact recursive principle to transformer key-value representations.

Agentic Engineering

Why This Transforms Agentic Coding

In an agentic harness, an agent repeatedly invokes tools across 10+ turns. Each turn resends 95% of the preceding conversation. In quadratic models, this creates massive redundant recomputation. KDA’s attention residual cache reuses prior loop activations directly, decreasing per-turn inference costs by up to 10×.
