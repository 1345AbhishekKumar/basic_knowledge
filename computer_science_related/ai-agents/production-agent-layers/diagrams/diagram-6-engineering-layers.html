Layer 04 · Loop Engineering

The Cross-Cutting Feedback Engine

Loop engineering defines what happens when an attempt fails. It is not confined to a single agent; it operates across 4 hierarchical tiers—from immediate tool retries (Loop 1) and evaluator graders (Loop 2) to event-driven updates (Loop 3) and multi-run hill-climbing optimization (Loop 4).
