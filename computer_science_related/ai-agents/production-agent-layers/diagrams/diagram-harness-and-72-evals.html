Harness Engineering

What is an Agent Harness?

The agent harness is the operational scaffolding wrapped around the model. It handles Pre-flight validation (sanitizing schemas, verifying auth), Runtime Governance (state management, circuit breakers, distributed tracing), and Post-flight compliance (auditing outputs before reaching end-users).

Evaluation Harness

The 72-Eval Suite for Enterprise Claims

To prevent catastrophic hallucinations (such as approving $4,500 on a $300 scratch), Maryam's insurance claim architecture runs 72 automated unit evals covering image mismatch detection, policy limit enforcement, risk score escalation, and immutable audit trails in CI/CD pipelines.
