The core problem

When inherited guarantees lose authority, what prevents freedom from becoming either despair or empty rebellion?

What you’ll learn

1–5 · The crisis and the answer

Death of God, nihilism, master/slave morality, will to power, and the overman.

6–9 · Affirmation and interpretation

Eternal recurrence, perspectivism, genealogy, and tragic art.

10–12 · Revaluation and new creators

Transvaluation, the critique of Christianity, free spirits, and future philosophers.

13–15 · Culture and the future

Democracy and mass culture, psychology/unconscious drives, and cultural cycles.

Learning rule

For every idea, ask: What does it produce in people and culture?

Final challenge

Can you connect individual psychology, values, culture, and creative achievement into one model?

Next steps

Read primary texts in this order: The Gay Science → Thus Spoke Zarathustra → Beyond Good and Evil → On the Genealogy of Morality → Twilight of the Idols. While reading, keep one notebook divided into four columns: value, psychological motive, cultural effect, and alternative.
